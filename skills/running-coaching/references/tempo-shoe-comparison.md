# Tempo shoe suitability, not an economy contest

Use when John asks which shoe would suit a completed tempo/threshold workout. First retrieve its exact date/activity/current shoe with `coach run-summary`; use the existing recommendation handler for actual inventory candidates, not a scratch query/script. See [the CLI owner](../../sportscoach-decisions/SKILL.md).

1. Establish actual workout intent, work-bout duration, source-correct intensity and surface. Pace alone does not define threshold: 4:00–4:45/km is not a universal physiological band.
2. Match historical mission/surface/pace/power-source conditions across the rotation. A roughly ±25 s/km screen may find candidates, but it does not establish a controlled comparison. Show sample count and whether duration, recovery/cooling and actual work structure match.
3. Load [construction](john-shoe-rotation.md), relevant pair dossiers and [personal evidence](shoe-rotation-evidence.md). Boston's glass-fibre EnergyRods are not a full carbon plate; unconfirmed Li-Ning stiffening is not carbon. Inventory and marketing cannot establish personal tolerance.
4. Compare power, HR and late-run cadence/GCT/stride stability only with provenance and conditions retained. Lower HR or GCT is not proof of shoe-induced economy or energy return. A longer stride alone does not prove a plate propelled the runner.
5. Qualify suitability, never impose categorical bans: Streakfly 2 can suit short race-specific quality work; Kinvara can suit uptempo work when its low-drop flexible geometry is tolerated; Red Hare 9 Ultra/Pro can serve faster steady work when personal evidence supports it. Match stability, symptoms and exposure rather than dismissing a construction class.
6. Give one directional pick when supported, its evidence tier, alternatives and limits. A recommendation does not change the actual worn-shoe assignment or authorize an import/correction.

For a stronger comparison, use repeated same-treadmill/route exposures, similar duration/recovery/cooling and a fixed pace or verified same-source power target. Compare late-run HR, source-correct power/pace, GCT stability, RPE and actual comfort/tendon reports. Choose intensity from current verified calibration and symptoms, not a historical 348 W target. Repeated crossover data improves inference; model-derived power still is not a physiological economy measurement.
