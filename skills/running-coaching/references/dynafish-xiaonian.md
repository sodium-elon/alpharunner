## Intended role

The Xiaonian is best classified as an **unplated performance daily trainer**: unusually light for its stack, cushioned enough for daily and long running, and capable of faster work. RTINGS rates it strongly for daily training, long runs, and half/full-marathon racing, while noting less bending stiffness and snap than a plated racer.

For John's rotation, start with this provisional hierarchy:

1. Easy/base familiar-surface trial.
2. Daily and long aerobic work after fit, stability, and tendon response are known.
3. Steady or faster work only after repeated tolerance and mechanics are clean.
4. Do not treat it as a stability shoe, medical intervention, or automatic replacement for a plated race shoe.

## First-run protocol

If current symptoms are settled, use a short easy run on a familiar surface. Keep the first exposure mechanically boring rather than testing the shoe's speed range. Record subjective fit and stability plus same-day and next-morning tendon response. A clean telemetry trace is useful but is not evidence of orthopedic safety.

## Comparison rules

- Compare matched conditions where possible: same surface/treadmill, similar duration, pace or power, cooling, and recovery state.
- Separate nominal product specifications from independent lab measurements.
- Never infer superior running economy from low mass, TPEE foam, rocker geometry, or review scores alone.
- Query live data before citing this dossier; AlphaRunner history outranks this static onboarding note.
