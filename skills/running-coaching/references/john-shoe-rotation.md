## Saucony Kinvara 16
- **Pair-canonical specification:** 29/25 mm stack, 4 mm offset, 210 g. Current Saucony regional/product listings may show 28/24 mm and 206 g, so retain the pair-specific AlphaRunner values and disclose the listing difference rather than oscillating between them.
- **Build:** lightweight, flexible neutral trainer; updated **PWRRUN** foam; no propulsion plate.
- **Role:** daily miles, easy-to-steady running, strides, and moderate uptempo work for a runner who tolerates low drop and wants ground feel.
- **Coaching lens:** low stack and 4 mm drop can shift relatively more demand toward calves/Achilles than high-stack, high-drop trainers. Do not treat light weight as fatigue protection.
- **Sources:** [Saucony product page](https://www.saucony.com/en/kinvara-16/60309M.html), [RunRepeat lab review](https://runrepeat.com/saucony-kinvara-16).

## Saucony Kinvara 15
- **Nominal specification:** 30/26 mm stack, 4 mm offset, about 190 g (men’s US 9).
- **Build:** lightweight neutral trainer; **PWRRUN** foam; no plate.
- **Role:** daily/steady work, strides, and uptempo running; not a maximal recovery cruiser.
- **Coaching lens:** compare with Kinvara 16 only at matched pace/power. Its low, flexible platform is useful for mechanics but remains a calf-load variable.
- **Sources:** [Doctors of Running](https://www.doctorsofrunning.com/2024/08/saucony-kinvara-15-review-2024.html), [RunRepeat lab review](https://runrepeat.com/saucony-kinvara-15).

## adidas Adizero Boston 13
- **Nominal specification:** 36/30 mm stack, 6 mm drop, about 257 g.
- **Build:** **Lightstrike Pro + Lightstrike 2.0** midsole and **glass-fibre EnergyRods 2.0**. The rods are not a full carbon plate.
- **Role:** durable fast trainer for long runs, marathon-pace blocks, tempo, and threshold work. Less natural for very easy plodding than a soft easy-day trainer.
- **Coaching lens:** rods and rocker can alter lower-leg loading and model-derived running power. Do not infer economy from raw all-run averages.
- **Sources:** [adidas product page](https://www.adidas.com/us/adizero-boston-13-shoes/JS4932.html), [RoadTrailRun](https://www.roadtrailrun.com/2025/04/adidas-adizero-boston-13-review-7.html), [Doctors of Running](https://www.doctorsofrunning.com/2026/02/adidas-adizero-boston-13-quick.html).

## adidas Adizero Evo SL
- **Nominal specification:** 38/32 mm stack, 6 mm drop, 224 g.
- **Build:** full-length **Lightstrike Pro**, small composite midfoot shank; no carbon plate and no EnergyRods.
- **Role:** lightweight, versatile performance trainer for easy-to-tempo running and long runs; not a stability shoe.
- **Coaching lens:** soft, energetic superfoam rather than plate-driven propulsion. Keep it analytically separate from Boston 13 despite the shared brand.
- **Sources:** [adidas product listing](https://www.adidas.com/om/en/adizero-evo-sl-shoes/KJ1364.html), [RunRepeat lab review](https://runrepeat.com/adidas-adizero-evo-sl), [Doctors of Running](https://www.doctorsofrunning.com/2025/01/adidas-adizero-evo-sl-review-2024.html).

## Li-Ning Red Hare 9 Ultra
- **Working specification:** commonly reported around 43/35 mm and 8 mm drop. A 260 g sample was reported in men’s US 10.5; other listings differ, so weight is not directly comparable with US 9 figures.
- **Build:** maximal super-trainer using **SUPER BOOM/BOOM** foam, GCU outsole, and a **PROBAR LOC** stabilising shank. Available descriptions indicate a small fiberglass/stiffening element, not a confirmed full-length carbon propulsion plate.
- **Role:** cushioned, lively long/easy-to-steady mileage; capable of marathon-pace work if stable and comfortable for John.
- **Coaching lens:** exact stack and plate language remain less authoritative than Nike/adidas/Saucony data. Do not call it carbon-plated. Use John’s 20+ run history rather than retailer mythology.
- **Sources:** [Supwell shoe data](https://www.supwell.com/shoes/li-ning-red-hare-9-ultra), [Supwell sample report](https://www.supwell.com/supbeat/li-ning-red-hare-9-ultra-the-most-fun-running-shoe-of-all-time), [RTINGS review](https://www.rtings.com/running-shoes/reviews/li-ning/red-hare-9-ultra).

## Li-Ning Red Hare 9 Pro

## Nike ZoomX Streakfly 2
- **Pair-canonical specification:** 26 mm heel stack, 4 mm offset, 128 g reference sample. Published sources also list roughly 27/23 mm and 128–144 g depending on sample size; treat the AlphaRunner value as canonical for John’s pair.
- **Build:** very light short-road racer with **ZoomX** foam and a **full-length carbon-fibre Flyplate**, built on a spike-like last.
- **Role:** mile–10K racing, track/road intervals, and sharp tempo work—not routine easy mileage or long slow runs.
- **Coaching lens:** a genuine carbon racer, but its low and aggressive geometry makes it a poor direct comparator with high-stack trainers. Limit early volume and watch calves/feet.
- **Sources:** [Nike product page](https://www.nike.com/t/streakfly-2-road-racing-shoes-I1ybhouy), [Nike newsroom](https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/vaporfly-4-streakfly-2-official-images), [RunRepeat lab review](https://runrepeat.com/nike-streakfly-2), [Doctors of Running](https://www.doctorsofrunning.com/2025/06/nike-streakfly-2-review-2025.html).
